Description

Project context

Tanacat is a 2D platformer created during Internal Game Jam GT Season 13. The game was intentionally designed around a simple single jump mechanic, making it easier for the team to focus on execution, level feel, and presentation within a very limited production time.

In this project, I explored the role of game designer more seriously. I was responsible for shaping the mechanics, preparing creative briefs, planning cutscene direction, and helping guide the team’s production flow. In practice, my role also involved mentoring juniors and making sure the concept stayed clear during development.
