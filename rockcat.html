Description

Project context

Rockcat: Operation Blue Heaven is a narrative side scrolling game that combines story progression with waste sorting mechanics. The project was created with my team to deliver a game experience that is both engaging and educational, especially around the topic of environmental awareness.

In this project, I worked primarily as the system programmer and handled most of the core gameplay implementation. My work covered player movement, UI systems, animation implementation, cutscene support, and NPC interaction systems that help drive the game’s progression.
